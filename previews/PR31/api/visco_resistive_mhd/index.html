Visco-resistive MHD boundary conditions

Boundary conditions for the visco-resistive MHD equations of Trixi.jl, i.e., IdealGlmMhdEquations2D for the hyperbolic part and ViscoResistiveMhd2D for the parabolic part: a slip wall with vanishing magnetic field (boundary_condition_slip_wall_no_magnetic_field) for the hyperbolic part and a wall with prescribed velocity, heat flux, and magnetic field (BoundaryConditionVRMHDWall with Isomagnetic) for the parabolic part. At outflow boundaries, the parabolic fluxes can be taken from the inside with boundary_condition_do_nothing of Trixi.jl. See the example p4est_2d_dgsem/elixir_vrmhd_vortex_street.jl.

AeroTrixi.BoundaryConditionVRMHDWall — Type
BoundaryConditionVRMHDWall(boundary_condition_velocity,
                           boundary_condition_heat_flux,
                           boundary_condition_magnetic)

Wall boundary condition for the parabolic part of the visco-resistive MHD equations ViscoResistiveMhd2D, analogous to BoundaryConditionNavierStokesWall of the compressible Navier-Stokes equations. It combines

  • a velocity boundary condition, currently NoSlip,
  • a heat flux boundary condition, currently Adiabatic,
  • a magnetic field boundary condition, currently Isomagnetic.

The combination NoSlip, Adiabatic, Isomagnetic is implemented for ViscoResistiveMhd2D with GradientVariablesPrimitive(). With prescribed boundary values of the velocity and the magnetic field, it can also be used at inflow boundaries, see the example p4est_2d_dgsem/elixir_vrmhd_vortex_street.jl.

AeroTrixi.boundary_condition_slip_wall_no_magnetic_field — Method
boundary_condition_slip_wall_no_magnetic_field(u_inner, normal_direction::AbstractVector,
                                               x, t, surface_flux_functions,
                                               equations::IdealGlmMhdEquations2D)

Slip wall boundary condition for the hyperbolic part of the (visco-resistive) MHD equations, which additionally imposes a vanishing magnetic field at the wall, e.g., for a non-magnetic body in a magnetized flow. The boundary state is the mirrored inner state, i.e., the normal velocity is reflected, while density, pressure, the out-of-plane velocity v3, and the divergence cleaning field psi are copied, and all components of the magnetic field are set to zero. The conservative and nonconservative surface fluxes are evaluated with the inner and the boundary state.

Use this boundary condition for the hyperbolic part together with a BoundaryConditionVRMHDWall with an Isomagnetic condition B = 0 for the parabolic part.
